HR is where you manage the people in your workshop, punch the attendance clock, and book leave. Shop opening hours and public holidays stay with workshop settings; they are not employee leave bookings.

## Add a person

Open **HR → Employees**. The roster uses the same people you assign to jobs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the employee record">
    Click **+ Employee**. Enter the name, role, sort order, and mother language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add HR details">
    Owners and administrators can add a **Hire date** and **Leave days**. Leave days is the person's yearly allowance.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link the login">
    The **Login** column shows whether an account is linked. The employee record and the login account are separate records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click a row to open **Employee Details**. Owners and administrators can enter **Carryover this year** and click **Save leave balance**. The **Remaining** value is the allowance plus carryover, less booked leave.

## Punch the attendance clock

Open **HR → Time Clock** to punch your own attendance. The page shows today's timeline in the workshop's local time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Come to work">
    Click **Come to work** when your shift starts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your clock state">
    Click **Pause** for a normal break, or **Doctor** when you leave for a doctor visit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish">
    Click **Go home** when your shift ends. **Come to work** resumes the clock after a pause or doctor visit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Owners and administrators can choose an employee on the Time Clock page and punch for that person. The team attendance table shows the selected day's events.

<Warning>
**Pause** on the attendance clock is not **Pause** on a job. Job Pause records why a task stopped; clock Pause records where the person is in their shift.
</Warning>

## Book leave

Open **HR → Leave**. Your **Remaining** chip shows the current balance for the selected year.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the booking form">
    Click **+ Leave**. Owners and administrators choose the employee; everyone else books their own leave.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the dates">
    Enter the **Start** and **End** dates. Add an optional note, then click **+ Leave** to book the range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the balance">
    Your booking appears in **My leave** with the date range, days charged, and status. The **Remaining** chip updates after the booking.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Owners, administrators, and service advisors can also view the **Team leave** month grid. Owners and administrators can click an empty employee/date cell to book leave for that person.

<Tip>
Public holidays that close the workshop do not use leave days. A short public holiday still counts as a workday when the workshop is open.
</Tip>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="No employee record linked">
    Ask an owner or administrator to link your login to an employee record under **HR → Employees**. Until it is linked, your Time Clock and Leave pages cannot load your personal record.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Cancel a booking">
    Right-click a booked row in **My leave** and choose **Cancel**. You can cancel your own leave when it starts today or later. Owners and administrators can cancel any booking.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Mechanic tablet">
    The mechanic **My Queue** has compact **Come to work**, **Pause**, **Doctor**, and **Go home** buttons in the header. They are attendance controls; job controls remain on the task.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
