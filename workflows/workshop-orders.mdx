---
title: "Workshop orders"
description: "Run the job: tasks, parts, notes, job card PDF, then checkout invoice."
---

**Workshop Orders** lists open work. Click the row for the full job. Top-right: **Pick Queue** and **+ Order**.

Order statuses: **SCHEDULED → INTAKE → IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED → INVOICED**.

Task statuses include **NOT_STARTED**, **IN_PROGRESS**, **WAITING_PARTS**, **DONE**. Mechanic pause can also show waiting for customer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the job">
    Header shows order number and status. Add tasks. Line items are parts and labor on each task. Notes and reported issue save inline until the job is **INVOICED**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print the job card">
    **Print Job Card** queues or downloads the PDF. Toast: **Generating Job Card PDF...**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Checkout">
    **Generate Invoice** is enabled when status is **COMPLETED** (or a linked invoice already exists). Inside checkout: **Create Draft Invoice**, then **Issue Invoice**. After issue the order is **INVOICED** and tasks lock.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Button | When it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Generate Invoice | Job completed, no invoice yet |
| Open Checkout | Draft invoice exists |
| Open Invoice | Already invoiced |
| Close Checkout | You are in checkout view |

<Warning>
You cannot delete tasks after the order is invoiced or already has a linked invoice. Labor time entries are never deleted.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Generate Invoice is greyed out">
    Finish the job first. Checkout only opens when status is **COMPLETED**, unless an invoice is already linked.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
