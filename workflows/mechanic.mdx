---
title: "Mechanic queue"
description: "Tablet flow: start the task, pause with a reason, request parts, photo, voice note, complete."
---

TECH users only see **My Queue**. Tap a row to open the task. Refresh is the icon top-right.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start or resume">
    **Start** on a new task. **Resume** if it was paused. **Switch Here** if another task already has the clock.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work">
    Diagnostics save on a short debounce. **Request Part** puts the job in a parts wait. Photos: jpeg, png, webp. Video: mp4 or QuickTime. Hold the voice control if the browser supports it; accept the draft after translation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pause or complete">
    **Pause** then pick **Waiting for Parts**, **Waiting for Customer**, or **Other**. **Confirm Pause**. **Complete** only while the task is active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Switch reasons: previous task waiting for parts, waiting for customer, or **Switched to Higher Priority**.

<Warning>
Voice notes need [Voice translation](/settings/voice-translation) credentials and a mother language on the [employee](/settings/employees). Without that, recording errors instead of a draft.
</Warning>
