---
title: "Pick queue"
description: "See which jobs still need parts pulled, then pick into the order."
---

From **Workshop Orders**, **Pick Queue** opens **Workshop Pick Queue**. Rows: order no., customer, vehicle, part lines, required qty, status. Click the row to open the pick drawer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the queue">
    Jobs with required part lines land here.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick">
    Open the row’s pick drawer. Pull quantities against the required lines. Stock moves through the inventory ledger.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
If required qty is zero, the job should not sit in the pick queue. Add part lines on the workshop order first.
</Check>
