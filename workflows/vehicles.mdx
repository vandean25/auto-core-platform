---
title: "Vehicles"
description: "Register the car, plate, and VIN against a customer before intake."
---

**Vehicles** lists every car in the tenant. Columns: vehicle, year, VIN, plate, customer. Top-right: **+ Vehicle**. Click the row for full vehicle context.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create from the list">
    **+ Vehicle** opens **Vehicle** dialog. Tie it to a customer. Unassigned shows as **Unassigned** on the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Or register at the door">
    [Workshop intake](/workflows/workshop-intake) **Quick Register** creates customer + vehicle when they are new.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
Intake search looks up VIN, plate, or customer name. Keep plate and VIN unique enough that the advisor hits the right car.
</Check>
