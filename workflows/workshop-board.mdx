---
title: "Workshop board"
description: "Drag jobs onto a mechanic or a bay so the floor knows who owns the stall."
---

**Workshop Board** is a kanban. Subtitle: drag and drop orders to assign mechanics and bays.

Header toggle: **By Mechanic** or **By Bay**. Empty columns stay empty until you create [employees](/settings/employees) or [bays](/settings/bays).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the view">
    **By Mechanic** or **By Bay**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop the card">
    Drag a workshop order onto a column, or use quick-assign on the card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
This assigns resources. It does not replace task start/complete on the mechanic tablet or the order detail page.
</Tip>
