---
title: "Customers"
description: "Open a private or company record before you write an order or start a job."
---

**Customers** is the CRM list. Click a row for the detail card (orders and vehicles hang off that person). Top-right: **+ Customer**. Search: **Search customers...** Right-click **Delete** when the record has no orders, invoices, jobs, or vehicles.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create">
    Choose private vs company in the dialog. Company uses **company name**. Private uses first and last name. Address, email, and phone live on the same form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the row">
    Detail shows history. From here you start a sales order with the customer already selected (`/sales-orders/new?customerId=…`).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Delete fails if the customer still owns a sales order, invoice, workshop order, or vehicle. The toast shows the server message.
</Warning>
