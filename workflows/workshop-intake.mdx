---
title: "Workshop intake"
description: "Find the car by VIN, plate, or name, then start a service — or quick-register a new customer."
---

**Workshop Intake** (`/workshop/intake`) is the door workflow. Top-right: **Quick Register**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search">
    **Intake Search**: VIN, plate, or customer name. Vehicles and matching customers show in two columns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Service">
    On a vehicle that has an owner, **Start Service** opens the start-service dialog and creates the job against that customer and car.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Nobody in the file">
    **Quick Register** captures a new customer and vehicle, then you can start service.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can also **+ Order** from [Workshop Orders](/workflows/workshop-orders) (intake dialog). Same job, different door.

<Check>
**Start Service** is disabled until the vehicle has a customer. Fix the vehicle record first.
</Check>
