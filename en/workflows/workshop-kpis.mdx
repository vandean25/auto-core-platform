---
title: "Workshop KPIs"
description: "Compare labor utilisation and parts turnover for one workshop site."
---

**Workshop KPIs** reports how much workshop time was available, clocked, and sold, and how parts inventory moved in a date range.

**OWNER**, **ADMIN**, and **SALES** roles see the sidebar entry. Other roles do not.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Workshop KPIs">
    Sidebar → **Workshop KPIs**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose site and period">
    Pick **Site**, **From**, **To**, and **Group by** (mechanic, week, or month).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export CSV">
    Click **Export CSV** to download labor performance, parts turnover, and slow-moving parts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Labor performance" icon="wrench">
    **Available** hours from schedules minus absences and full-day holidays. **Utilisation** is clocked ÷ available. **Productivity** is sold hours from finalized invoice lines ÷ clocked hours. **Avg. net/order** divides net revenue on finalized workshop invoices by closed orders.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Parts turnover" icon="package">
    **Issued cost** from workshop consumption in the period. **Avg. stock value** averages daily valued stock. **Turnover** is issued cost ÷ average stock. Up to ten parts with no consumption in 90 days appear as slow movers.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Check>
You only see sites where you have an active site membership. Date boundaries use the selected site time zone. A single report spans at most 366 calendar days.
</Check>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Open labor time entries">
    Time entries without an end time do not count toward clocked hours. The report shows open entry counts separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Schedule version changes">
    Each day uses the schedule version that starts on that date. The site on that version decides which site receives available hours.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Blank percentages or stock value">
    When available or clocked hours are zero, utilisation and productivity stay empty. Unvalued stock is not guessed; the report lists unvalued items separately.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="CSV format">
    The export uses semicolon separators and dot decimals. It includes labor rows, a total row, parts turnover summary, slow-mover lines, and metadata (time zone, grouping, unassigned mechanics).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
