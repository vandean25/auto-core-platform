---
title: "Accounting export"
description: "Close a period, clear export blockers, and download DATEV, RZL, or BMD CSV runs."
---

Accounting export turns finalized invoices and credit notes into immutable CSV files for your accountant system. The product supports three profile codes on the legal entity: **DATEV** (`ACP-DATEV-DE-EUR-1`, Germany), **RZL** (`ACP-RZL-AT-EUR-1`, Austria), and **BMD** (`ACP-BMD-AT-EUR-1`, Austria). RZL and BMD may stay disabled until your platform operator releases them in that environment.

Only **OWNER** and **ADMIN** members with complete site access can preview or generate exports.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Export Readiness" icon="clipboard-check" href="/en/settings/finance#export-readiness">
    See blocked documents before you generate a file.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Legal entity profile" icon="building-2" href="/en/settings/legal-entities">
    DATEV, RZL, or BMD mapping and enable toggles per entity.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Clear Export Readiness">
    Open **Export Readiness** in the sidebar. Pick the legal entity and period. Fix every blocked invoice or credit note.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Accounting export">
    **Settings** (gear) → **Finance** → sub-tab **Accounting export**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview">
    Choose the legal entity, **Date from**, and **Date to**. Click **Preview**. Resolve any **Blocking issues** the preview lists.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate and download">
    Click **Generate**, then **Download** or **Download latest** from the run history. Downloads are audit-logged; they do not confirm import into DATEV, RZL, or BMD.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
The on-screen copy references DATEV Buchungsstapel CSV because that is the default profile in the demo UI. Austrian entities with **RZL export enabled** or **BMD** profile `ACP-BMD-AT-EUR-1` (when released) use the same preview and generate flow; the downloaded filename and column layout follow the entity profile code.
</Tip>

<Warning>
Importing overlapping CSV files into your accounting system can duplicate bookings. The export screen warns when a period overlaps prior runs. Acknowledge the risk before **Generate anyway**.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="BMD vs RZL vs DATEV">
    | Profile | Typical use | UI configuration |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | `ACP-DATEV-DE-EUR-1` | German DATEV Buchungsstapel | **DATEV export enabled** toggle on the DE legal entity |
    | `ACP-RZL-AT-EUR-1` | Austrian RZL Fibu import | **RZL export configuration** (firm number, Steuercodes) on the AT legal entity |
    | `ACP-BMD-AT-EUR-1` | Austrian BMD NTCS import | Profile code on the accounting profile; CSV generation is gated until the operator enables BMD in that environment |
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Export refused">
    If preview shows a scope refusal instead of document totals, your account lacks active membership on a site that holds documents in the period. An owner or admin must fix **Settings → Sites** and team site access.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
