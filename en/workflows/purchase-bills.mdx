---
title: "Purchase bills"
description: "Capture the vendor’s invoice number, post it, then mark it paid."
---

**Purchase Bills** tracks what you owe. Top-right: **+ Bill**. **Bill #** is the vendor’s invoice number. Click the row.

On the list, a posted unpaid bill shows as **Unpaid** (status **POSTED**). After payment it shows **PAID**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft">
    **+ Bill**, or **Create Bill** from a purchase order. A **DRAFT** opens the form. It auto-saves. The vendor invoice number is what you file against their paper.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Post Bill">
    **Post Bill** asks to confirm. Posting locks the bill for editing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark as Paid">
    On a posted bill, green **Mark as Paid**. Toast: **Bill marked as paid**.
  </Step>
</Steps>
