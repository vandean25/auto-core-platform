---
title: "Purchase orders"
description: "Send the PO, receive parts to the right stock location, then bill only what arrived."
---

**Purchase Orders**: **+ Purchase Order**. Statuses: **DRAFT → SENT → PARTIAL → COMPLETED**. Right-click **Delete** on **DRAFT** only.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Draft">
    Pick a [vendor](/workflows/vendors), add lines (catalog item, qty, price).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark as Sent">
    On **DRAFT**, top-right **Mark as Sent**. That is the office saying it left the building.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Goods">
    Until **COMPLETED**, **Receive Goods**. Partial receipts move status to **PARTIAL**. Lines reserved for a workshop job go straight to that job's staging tote, not a free-stock bin. Unlinked items use the free-stock bin provided, or the default general bin; released linked items require a free-stock bin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Bill">
    **Create Bill (N items)** uses unbilled received qty and jumps to a new vendor bill with vendor and PO prefilled. Disabled when the unbilled count is 0.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
You cannot receive after **COMPLETED**. Fiscal lock still applies to dated receipts and bills.
</Warning>
