---
title: "Credit notes"
description: "Issue credit notes (Gutschriften) against finalized invoices and download the PDF."
---

Use **Credit Notes** in the sidebar when you need to reduce or reverse part of a finalized tax invoice without editing the original document. Credit notes get their own number series and appear in [Export Readiness](/en/settings/finance#export-readiness) before accounting export.

Only **OWNER** and **ADMIN** members see the **Credit Notes** sidebar entry.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Credit Notes">
    In the sidebar, click **Credit Notes** (*Gutschriften*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create from an invoice">
    Open a finalized invoice from [Invoices](/en/workflows/invoices) and click **Credit note**. Choose full or partial credit, then create the draft. You can also open existing rows from the **Credit Notes** list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize and download">
    Complete the reason and lines, **Finalize** the credit note, then **Download PDF** like a tax invoice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Credit #** | Assigned number after finalize, or a short draft id |
| **Date** | Document date |
| **Reason** | Free-text reason stored on the credit note |
| **Total** | Gross total |

<Warning>
Cancelled invoices cannot be exported. A credit note that blocks export readiness must be fixed or voided before you generate an accounting CSV for that period.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can use this">
    Sidebar visibility is limited to **OWNER** and **ADMIN**. Advisors and mechanics do not see the module.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Accounting export">
    Credit notes use the same readiness rules as invoices. See [Finance](/en/settings/finance#export-readiness) and [Accounting export](/en/workflows/accounting-export).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
