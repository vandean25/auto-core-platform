---
title: "Vendors"
description: "Name the supplier and which brands they actually sell you."
---

**Vendors** lists suppliers. Top-right: **+ Vendor**. Click the row for brands and orders. Right-click **Delete** only when there are no purchase orders, purchase bills, or vehicle purchases linked to the vendor.

On the detail card you attach [brands](/settings/brands) the vendor supports. That list feeds purchasing, not the customer catalog.

<Check>
Create the vendor before the first [purchase order](/workflows/purchase-orders). The PO header is vendor + lines.
</Check>
