---
title: "Workshop capacity"
description: "Week-by-week bay and labor capacity before you commit planner appointments."
---

**Workshop Capacity** shows how much labor and bay time you have free across weeks. Use it before you overload the [Workshop Planner](/en/workflows/workshop-planner).

Planners, advisors, and admins use it most. **OWNER**, **ADMIN**, and **SALES** roles can open **Workshop KPIs**; capacity is visible to all workshop roles.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Workshop Capacity">
    Sidebar → **Workshop Capacity**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick site and horizon">
    Choose the site (from your memberships) and the week range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read free vs booked">
    Compare available hours from schedules and holidays against existing planner bookings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust planner">
    Move or shorten appointments in the planner when a week shows zero slack.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Related reports">
    Historical utilisation is in [Workshop KPIs](/en/workflows/workshop-kpis). Capacity is forward-looking; KPIs are backward-looking.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
