---

**Workshop Board** (`/workshop/board`) is a kanban for live floor dispatch. Subtitle: drag and drop orders to assign mechanics and bays.

To check future bay availability or book incoming appointments against the clock, use the [Workshop Planner](/workflows/workshop-planner) instead.

Header toggle: **By Mechanic** or **By Bay**. Empty columns stay empty until you create [employees](/settings/employees) or [bays](/settings/bays).

Employee leave lives in [HR](/hr#book-leave), not on the workshop board.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the view">
    **By Mechanic** or **By Bay**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drop the card">
    Drag a workshop order onto a column, or use quick-assign on the card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
This assigns resources for active floor work. It does not replace task start/complete on the mechanic tablet or the order detail page.
</Tip>
