---
title: "Tyre storage"
description: "Store customer tyre sets, log check-in and check-out, and see what is due."
---

**Tyre storage** (*Reifenlager*) tracks seasonal tyre sets per customer vehicle: location in your warehouse, status events, and due lists for seasonal changeover.

Advisors and parts staff use it daily. Mechanics may view sets linked to vehicles they work on.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Tyre storage">
    Sidebar → **Tyre storage**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or open a set">
    Start a new set from a customer vehicle or open an existing row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check in tyres">
    Record **check-in** with storage location and season label. The set status updates from events.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check out or move">
    **Check-out** when the customer takes tyres back. Use move events when bins change inside the warehouse.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
You cannot check in the same set twice without an intervening check-out. The API rejects double check-in.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Due lists">
    Dashboard widgets and list filters highlight sets that are due for seasonal swap. Pair with [Workshop orders](/en/workflows/workshop-orders) when you book the appointment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Storage locations">
    Bin codes come from [Storage locations](/en/settings/storage-locations). Create the tree before you assign precise bin positions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
