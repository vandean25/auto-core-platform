---
title: "Statutory warranty due"
description: "See which sold vehicles approach Gewährleistung deadlines."
---

**Statutory warranty due** (*Gewährleistung*) is a list view for dealer stock and sold units whose statutory warranty period is ending soon. Use it to proactively contact buyers or plan goodwill before disputes.

All roles see the sidebar module. Warranty claim paperwork on a job still lives under [Warranty and goodwill](/en/workflows/warranty-claims).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Statutory warranty due">
    Sidebar → **Statutory warranty due**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review deadlines">
    Scan due dates, customer, and vehicle identity columns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the vehicle">
    Click through to the vehicle record. The **Gewährleistung** section shows term start, duration, and computed end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create follow-up work">
    Book a workshop order or sales touchpoint from your normal modules when you need shop work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Vehicle sales">
    Warranty terms are set when you complete a [vehicle stock](/en/workflows/vehicle-stock) sale. Missing sale date or term length keeps vehicles off this list.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
