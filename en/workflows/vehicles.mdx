**Vehicles** lists every car in the tenant. Columns: vehicle, year, VIN, plate, customer. Top-right: **+ Vehicle**. Click the row for full vehicle context.

### Registration & emissions (Zulassung & Emissionen)

On **+ Vehicle**, **Edit Vehicle**, and the vehicle detail card you can capture Austrian regulatory data (all optional):

- First registration date / Erstzulassung
- CO₂ WLTP and optional NEDC (g/km)
- Typenschein number (type approval)
- NoVA class (used by the live NoVA preview calculator)
- Emission class / Schadstoffklasse

On the vehicle detail view, the **NoVA** panel calculates a preview from registration data, CO₂, Typenschein, class, and net price. **Calculate** calls the server; nothing is saved to the vehicle or any invoice. Your tax advisor still confirms the amount before you rely on it for registration.

**ZB I vs Typenschein:** The existing **registration certificate** field on dealer stock vehicles is the ZB I (Fahrzeugschein). The **Typenschein** is the separate type-approval document — use the Typenschein field for that number.

  </Step>
</Steps>
