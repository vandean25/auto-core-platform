---
title: "Warranty and goodwill"
description: "Open a Garantie/Kulanz claim file on a workshop order and track claim types."
---

The **Warranty / goodwill** tab on a workshop order holds claim records for manufacturer warranty (*Garantie*), goodwill (*Kulanz*), and statutory warranty (*Gewährleistung*) workflows tied to that job.

Advisors, admins, and owners use it during the job. Mechanics see the order but manage claims from the advisor-facing tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workshop order">
    **Workshop Orders** → click the row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Warranty / goodwill">
    In the order navigation, open **Warranty / goodwill** (route suffix `garantie-kulanz`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a claim">
    Click **Warranty claim**. Pick the claim type (**Warranty**, **Goodwill**, or **Statutory warranty**) and fill the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track on the order">
    Claims stay linked to the order for audit and handover to the back office.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
Statutory warranty *due dates* for sold vehicles are tracked separately on **Statutory warranty due** — see [Statutory warranty due](/en/workflows/statutory-warranty-due).
</Check>
