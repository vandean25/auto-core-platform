---
title: "Listings export"
description: "Export vehicle stock ads to mobile.de, willhaben, or AutoScout24."
---

The listing editor on a **vehicle stock** unit prepares marketplace fields, equipment mapping, and consumer hints (including NoVA wording). Export generates portal-specific files you upload or push according to each portal’s process.

Sales and admins manage listings. Advisors can help with photos and descriptions.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open stock detail">
    **Vehicle Stock** → click the unit → open the **Listing** editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete required fields">
    Fill title, price, mileage, equipment, and confirm **NoVA included** when the ad must state tax treatment (*inkl. USt. und NoVA* in German copy). Under **Photos for the listing**, reorder images and select only the photos that should ship in the export bundle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose portal profile">
    Select the target portal (for example **mobile.de**, **willhaben**, or **AutoScout24**). Equipment codes map through ACP’s portal tables.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export">
    Run **Export** and download the generated file or follow the in-app push flow when AutoScout24 DMS API credentials are configured for your tenant.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Portal acceptance is yours to verify. ACP maps known equipment codes; unmapped options export as free text and may need manual cleanup in the portal UI.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can use this">
    Anyone who can edit vehicle stock economics and marketing fields can export. API push requires operator-level credentials outside this guide.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
