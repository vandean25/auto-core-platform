---
title: "Workshop orders"
description: "Run the job: tasks, parts, notes, job card PDF, then checkout invoice."
---

**Workshop Orders** lists open work. Click a row for a **peek** sheet; **Open in full** or **O** with row focus opens the full job. Right-click a row for quick actions (e.g. **Vehicle check-in**, **Job card PDF**, **Assign mechanic**, task **Done** / **Reopen task**) when your role and status allow. The same actions appear in **Global Search** under **Current record** while the order is open. Top-right: **Parts pick queue** and **+ Order**.

Order statuses: **SCHEDULED → INTAKE → IN_PROGRESS → COMPLETED → INVOICED**. Appointments booked in the [Workshop Planner](/en/workflows/workshop-planner) start in **SCHEDULED** holding the bay, then advance to **INTAKE** when the car arrives.

Task statuses include **NOT_STARTED**, **IN_PROGRESS**, **WAITING_PARTS**, **DONE**. Mechanic pause can also show waiting for customer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the job">
    Header shows order number and status. Add tasks. Line items are parts and labor on each task. Notes and reported issue save inline until the job is **INVOICED**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print the job card">
    **Print Job Card** queues or downloads the PDF. Toast: **Generating Job Card PDF...**
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cost estimate (Kostenvoranschlag)">
    OWNER, ADMIN, and SALES see the **Estimate** card while the job is in **INTAKE** or **IN_PROGRESS**. **Create estimate** snapshots tasks and lines; **Send to customer** emails a PDF and optional notification. The customer opens the public approval link (`/approve/:token`), reviews totals, and approves or declines. **Record confirmation** captures phone or in-person approval when they do not use the link. Revisions create a new draft version; overrun warnings appear when work exceeds the approved total.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Checkout">
    **Generate Invoice** is enabled when status is **COMPLETED** (or a linked invoice already exists). Inside checkout: **Create Draft Invoice**, then **Issue Invoice**. After issue the order is **INVOICED** and tasks lock.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ready-for-pickup email">
    When every task is **DONE**, the order becomes **COMPLETED** and the platform queues one transactional ready-for-pickup email to the customer (no marketing; language follows the customer or tenant default, typically German or English), if the customer has a confirmed email for order communication. The **Customer communication** card shows delivery state. Advisors can **Resend** when contact details were missing at completion or delivery failed; mechanics cannot send or resend.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Button | When it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Generate Invoice | Job completed, no invoice yet |
| Open Checkout | Draft invoice exists |
| Open Invoice | Already invoiced |
| Close Checkout | You are in checkout view |

<Warning>
You cannot delete tasks after the order is invoiced or already has a linked invoice. Labor time entries are never deleted.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Generate Invoice is greyed out">
    Finish the job first. Checkout only opens when status is **COMPLETED**, unless an invoice is already linked.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
