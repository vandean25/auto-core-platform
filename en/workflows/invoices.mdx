---
title: "Invoices"
description: "Read a tax invoice, download the PDF, and know when the number is real."
---

Open **Invoices** in the sidebar (`/sales/invoices`) to browse drafts and finalized tax invoices. You can also open an invoice from a [sales order](/en/workflows/sales-orders), from workshop checkout, or from **Ctrl+K → Find a sales order to invoice** (which opens the sales order list).

The header shows the invoice number (or a short id while still draft) and a status badge: **DRAFT**, **FINALIZED**, **ISSUED**, **PAID**, **CANCELLED**.

Top-right: **Download PDF**. That generates the PDF and downloads it. If the server queues it, the toast says generation is in the background — try Download PDF again shortly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="From a sales order">
    **Create Invoice** on the order. See [Sales orders](/en/workflows/sales-orders).
  </Step>
  <Step title="From a completed job">
    On the workshop order, **Generate Invoice** / checkout. Issue the draft there. See [Workshop orders](/en/workflows/workshop-orders).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the PDF">
    **Download PDF** on the invoice. Filename is based on the invoice number. Finalizing an invoice does not download anything, so this is the step that saves a copy.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Numbers look like `RE-2026-0001` (year + four digits). Line **revenue group names** and unit prices are snapshotted at issue. Changing a [revenue group](/en/settings/revenue-groups) later does not rewrite old invoices.

<Tip>
Need who changed a draft line yesterday? [Audit logs](/en/settings/audit-logs), not the PDF.
</Tip>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Find a sales order to invoice from search">
    **Ctrl+K → Find a sales order to invoice** opens the sales order list. Open an order and use **Create Invoice** to start a sourced draft, then edit and finalize from the draft editor. Standalone blank invoices are not supported.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
