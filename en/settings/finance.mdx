Use **Settings → Finance** when the books for a period must stay closed, or when you need to see how this tenant stores invoice prefix and next number.

This tab is one form. Changes sit in the fields until you click **Save Changes**.

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Fiscal Control" icon="calendar-lock" href="#lock-date">
    Block edits to transactions on or before the lock date.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Invoice Numbering" icon="hash" href="#invoice-numbering">
    Prefix and next-number fields on the finance record.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Export Readiness" icon="clipboard-check" href="#export-readiness">
    See which documents in a period can be exported, and why the rest are held back.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Click the gear in the app header.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stay on Finance">
    Finance is the first tab. If you landed on another tab, click **Finance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit, then Save Changes">
    The save button is bottom-right of the form. A toast reads **Settings updated** when it sticks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Lock Date

**Lock Date** is a date picker. Helper text on the screen: transactions on or before this date cannot be modified.

Leave it empty if you do not want a lock.

<Warning>
Moving the lock **later** opens **Confirm Fiscal Lock**. Confirming permanently blocks changes in the previous period and can affect finalized reports. Cancel if you are not ready. The product does not reopen a closed period from this screen.
</Warning>

| You do this | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Pick a date and save | Dates on or before that day are locked for this tenant |
| Pick a **later** date than today | Confirm Fiscal Lock, then **Confirm Lock** |
| Clear the date and save | No lock date |

## Invoice Numbering

Two fields sit side by side:

| Field | What you type |
| --- | --- |
| **Prefix** | Text stored on finance settings (seed default looks like `RE-2026-`) |
| **Next Number** | Integer stored on finance settings (seed default `1001`) |

<Tip>
When you **finalize** a tax invoice, ACP assigns `RE-` plus the current year plus a four-digit yearly counter (`RE-2026-0001`). Already issued numbers do not change when you edit these fields.
</Tip>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What this tab does not show">
    Sales order prefixes (`SO-…`) and workshop order prefixes (`WO-…`) live on the same finance record in the database. They are not fields on this tab. Fiscal year start month is also not on this screen.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who sees this">
    Settings is tenant-wide. There is no separate finance login. Changes apply to this workshop tenant only.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Export Readiness

Export Readiness is a page in the sidebar, not a tab on this screen. Open it before you run an accounting export. For one legal entity and one period, it shows which invoices and credit notes can go out, and which are held back with the reason for each.

Owners and admins see the link. Other roles do not.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Export Readiness">
    Click **Export Readiness** in the sidebar, under **Credit Notes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the legal entity and period">
    Pick the legal entity, then set **From** and **To**. The page starts on the previous calendar month.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the cards">
    **Exportable** counts documents with no blocking reason. **Blocked** counts documents with at least one. **Period** reads **Closed** when the lock date covers the period end.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a blocked document">
    Click its row. The invoice or credit note opens, so you can fix the cause there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click a reason under the cards to show only the documents it blocks. **Reason** and **Document type** narrow the list the same way. The search box matches the document number, type, status, date, and site. The date matches as the list shows it (for example `05 Mar 2026`) or as `2026-03-05`.

Click a column header to sort the whole period, not only the page you are on. The Reasons column cannot be sorted.

<Warning>
One blocked document stops the export for that legal entity and period. Clear every blocked row, then generate the CSV in **Settings → Finance → Accounting export** (DATEV, RZL, or BMD profile per legal entity — see [Accounting export](/en/workflows/accounting-export)).
</Warning>

<Tip>
If the legal entity has no accounting export profile, or the profile is turned off, a notice appears above the list. Set up or turn on the profile in **Settings** before you export. Until then, no period for that entity can be exported.
</Tip>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Reasons on a blocked document">
    | Reason | What it means |
    | --- | --- |
    | Legacy document | The document was saved before the current invoice format, so its accounting data cannot be checked. |
    | Outside DE export | A line's accounting allocation is for a country other than Germany (DE). Only German lines can be exported right now. |
    | Unsupported tax mode | The document uses margin-scheme tax, which the export profile does not support. |
    | Mapping incomplete | A line has no accounting allocation saved on it. |
    | Ownership evidence missing | The document is missing its legal entity, site, or number. |
    | Cancelled | Cancelled documents cannot be exported. |
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Notices above the list">
    Some blocks belong to the whole period, not to one row. A document with no legal entity is one example. These appear as a notice above the list, because no single row can show them. The notice does not name the document.

    A document that has a legal entity but no site is a row with the **Ownership evidence missing** reason. Its gap also appears in the notice above the list. A cancelled document shows only **Cancelled** on its row, so its ownership gap appears only in the notice.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Refusals">
    The page shows a refusal message instead of the cards and the list when it cannot show the period. That happens when a site that holds documents in the period is inactive, or when you have no active site membership for one of those sites. Ask an owner or admin to give you access to the site, or reactivate an inactive site under **Settings → Sites**. Then reload this page.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
