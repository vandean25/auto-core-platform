---
title: "Team"
description: "Invite people who can sign in, and change their role without mixing them up with workshop employees."
---

Use **Settings → Team** for **login membership**. You only see this tab if your role is **OWNER** or **ADMIN**. Everyone else is sent back to Finance.

These are not the names on [Employees](/settings/employees). Invite an email here; assign a mechanic over there.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Invite">
    **+ Member** → **Invite Member**. Email required. Role: OWNER, ADMIN, TECH, or SALES (default TECH). **Invite Member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a person">
    Click the row. **Member Details** sheet opens on the right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change role or access">
    Role is inline edit (`OWNER`, `ADMIN`, `TECH`, `SALES`). Footer: **Deactivate Member** or **Reactivate Member**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## List

| Column | Content |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | First + last, or the email if no name |
| **Role** | OWNER, ADMIN, TECH, SALES |
| **Status** | Active / Inactive badge |

Search: **Search team members...**

<Warning>
There is no delete on this tab. Deactivate access. Role and status changes refresh the person’s permissions immediately.
</Warning>

<Accordion title="Invite of someone who already existed">
Inviting the same email can reactivate a membership instead of creating a duplicate. A failed load shows a rose banner with **Retry**.
</Accordion>
