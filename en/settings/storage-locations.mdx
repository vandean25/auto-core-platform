---
title: "Storage locations"
description: "Build the warehouse tree so stock has a bin, not a mystery pile."
---

Use **Settings → Storage Locations** to name warehouses and the aisles, shelves, and bins under them. Stock quantities live on bins, not on this screen.

Left: the tree. Right: **Add Location**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the warehouse first">
    Type **Warehouse**. Parent is locked to **None (Root)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hang children underneath">
    Type Aisle, Shelf, or Bin. Pick a **Parent Location**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give a unique Code">
    Codes show as a small badge on the tree (example `WH-001`).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add Location

| Field | Rules |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Required. Example: Main Warehouse |
| **Code (Unique)** | Required |
| **Type** | Warehouse, Aisle, Shelf, Bin |
| **Parent Location** | Disabled for Warehouse. For other types, pick an existing row |

**Create Location** sits at the bottom of the right card.

## Tree

Click a row with children to expand or collapse. Hover shows a red trash on the right. Confirm **Delete {name}?**

<Warning>
Delete is allowed only when the location has **no children** and **no stock**. The server message is the source of truth. Soft-delete is used; the name should not come back as a live bin.
</Warning>

<Accordion title="Types you will not pick here">
The product also knows customer storage and staging totes for workshop flows. This Settings form only creates warehouse, aisle, shelf, and bin.
</Accordion>
