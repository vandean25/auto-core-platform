---
title: "Sites"
description: "Create workshop sites, link legal entities, and control who sees each location."
---

Use **Settings → Sites** to define every physical workshop (*Standort*) under the tenant. Sites drive the header site switcher, stock scope, and invoice site evidence for accounting export.

Only **OWNER** and **ADMIN** members see this tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sites">
    Gear → **Sites**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a site">
    Add the display name, code, and linked [legal entity](/en/settings/legal-entities). Country follows the entity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign members">
    Grant site membership to team accounts that should see operational data for that location.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deactivate carefully">
    Deactivating a site blocks operational views that still hold documents there. Prefer fixing export blockers before you deactivate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Users without membership on a site cannot preview accounting exports that include documents from that site.
</Warning>

<Tip>
Daily switching happens in the app header — see [Sites and site switcher](/en/workflows/sites).
</Tip>
