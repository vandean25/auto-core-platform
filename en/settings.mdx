title: "Settings"
description: "Every workshop control plane lives behind the gear: books, people, bins, and the audit trail."
---

Click the **gear** in the app header. Settings is one tabbed page for this tenant (17 tabs). There is no separate admin site. Several tabs (legal entities, sites, team, API keys, margin rules, data import, and the Finance **Accounting export** sub-tab) are visible only to **OWNER** and **ADMIN**.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Finance" icon="banknote" href="/en/settings/finance">

  </Card>

  <Card title="Legal entities" icon="building-2" href="/en/settings/legal-entities">

  </Card>

  <Card title="Document branding" icon="file-image" href="/en/settings/document-branding">
    Letterhead and PDF layout per legal entity.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Revenue groups" icon="percent" href="/en/settings/revenue-groups">
    Tax buckets snapshotted onto invoice lines.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Sites" icon="map-pin" href="/en/settings/sites">
    Workshop sites, legal-entity linkage, and memberships (OWNER/ADMIN).
  </Card>

  <Card title="Voice translation" icon="languages" href="/en/settings/voice-translation">
    Target language and Google credentials for mechanic voice notes.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Brands" icon="tag" href="/en/settings/brands">

  </Card>

  </Card>

<Tip>
  **Employees** are workshop roster names. **Team** members are login accounts. Mixing them up is the usual first mistake.
</Tip>
