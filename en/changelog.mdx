Recent releases, grouped by week. For step-by-step guides, use the role cards on the [welcome page](/en/introduction).

<Update label="October 13–18, 2026" description="Language, check-in, and listings">
  **German and English in the app.** You can switch the interface language. Customer-facing PDFs and emails can follow the customer’s language where configured.

  **Tablet vehicle check-in.** Capture arrival photos, damage on the vehicle sketch, intake checklist, and customer signature. Check-in can feed the workshop order and PDF.

  **Used-vehicle listings.** Add photos and equipment, edit listings, and export files for marketplaces (including mobile.de, willhaben, and AutoScout24 CSV where enabled).

  **Workshop and parts.** Loaner cars can appear as a billable line on an order. Inventory item details support receive, adjust, and edit. Workshop board improvements for smaller screens.

  **Pickerl (§57a).** Import inspection results and brake measurements into the vehicle record when your process supplies them.

  **Contact preferences.** Customer consent is checked before outbound messages leave the system.

  **Data import.** Labor-operations CSV, first registration date on vehicle import, and purge of old import row payloads after 180 days.

  **Registration scan at intake.** Upload certificate front/back, review fields, and jump straight into tablet check-in.

  **Documentation.** Stale help text on several workflow pages was corrected; workshop KPIs are back in the menu.
</Update>

<Update label="October 6–12, 2026" description="Invoices, estimates, and workshop planning">
  **Invoices in the sidebar.** Open **Invoices** from the main menu. Finalize an invoice, then use **Download PDF** for the customer copy.

  **Estimates (Kostenvoranschlag).** Build estimates with numbering and PDFs; send them for customer approval and track decisions on the order.

  **Workshop capacity.** Week view of bays and scheduled hours to see load before you promise a date.

  **Onboarding checklist.** New admins see a short checklist in the app, plus a **Help / Documentation** link in the sidebar.

  **Command palette and tables.** Search more modules by name, export list views to CSV, and pick visible columns on supported tables.

  **Accounting export readiness.** See per legal entity whether a period is ready to export and what still blocks it.
</Update>

<Update label="September 29 – October 5, 2026" description="AT compliance and vehicle sales">
  **Pickerl due dates.** Due list and dashboard widget; create a §57a workshop order from a due vehicle in one step.

  **NoVA.** Calculator and preview on the vehicle for importers and sales staff.

  **Tyre storage.** Store customer tyre sets, log movements, and see what is due back.

  **Loaner fleet.** Manage replacement vehicles available for customer jobs.

  **Vehicle stock and sales.** Track stock age and margin, warranty periods on sold vehicles, purchase contract PDFs, and trade-in handling on sales invoices.

  **Invoice branding.** Letterhead and branding on invoice PDFs when your legal entity is configured.

  **Credit notes.** Issue credit notes with their own PDF download flow, alongside sales invoices.
</Update>

<Update label="September 22–28, 2026" description="Finance hardening and workshop KPIs">
  **Invoice and credit-note PDFs.** More reliable download after finalize, including when PDFs are generated in the background.

  **Finalize checks.** Clearer messages when stock or rules block finalizing an invoice.

  **Workshop KPI report.** Labour utilisation, parts turnover, and slow-moving stock for a site and date range (export to CSV).

  **Differenzbesteuerung.** Correct legal note on margin-scheme invoices for German rules.

  **Sites and transfers.** Move stock between locations; create transfers with clearer **New transfer** labelling in the UI.
</Update>

<Update label="August 25 – September 21, 2026" description="Foundation for DACH workshops">
  **Seller identity on invoices.** Legal entity, tax data, and revenue groups wired through to invoice drafts.

  **Accounting export (DATEV and related).** Export hub and settings for handing periods to your accountant.

  **Credit notes and storno flow.** Credit note numbering and documents alongside invoices.

  **Multi-site.** Switch site in the app; data and stock respect the active location.

  **Parts and purchasing.** Reserve parts on jobs, raise purchase orders from demand, and receive goods into inventory.

  **Data import.** CSV wizard for customers and vehicles when you move from a legacy system.

  **Agent supervision (early).** Read-only views for monitored automation proposals — mainly for admins evaluating agent features.
</Update>
