Auto Core Platform is a workshop and dealer management system built for Austria and the wider DACH region. It keeps customers, vehicles, jobs, parts, purchases, and invoices in one place — with a change history you can trust when someone asks *who changed this?*

Pick your role below. Each card links to the guides that match that workday.
