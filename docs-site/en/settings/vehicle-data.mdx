---
title: "Vehicle data"
description: "Configure VIN decode and catalog providers for fitment and vehicle intelligence."
---

**Settings → Vehicle data** controls how ACP resolves VINs and which catalog provider backs part fitment and vehicle attributes.

Only **OWNER** and **ADMIN** members see this tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Vehicle data">
    Gear → **Vehicle data**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose providers">
    Select the active VIN and catalog providers your contract includes. Save changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test on a vehicle">
    Open [Vehicles](/en/workflows/vehicles), create or edit a record, and run VIN decode to confirm results.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Gotchas">
    - Provider credentials are tenant-wide. A wrong provider shows empty fitment in parts search.
    - Decoded data never replaces your legal responsibility to verify plate and registration documents at intake.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
