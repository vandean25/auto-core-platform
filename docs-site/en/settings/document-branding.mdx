---
title: "Document branding"
description: "Upload letterhead per legal entity so PDFs match your workshop stationery."
---

**Document branding** controls how generated PDFs (invoices, job cards, and other letterhead-backed documents) look for each [legal entity](/en/settings/legal-entities). You upload a PNG or PDF letterhead, optionally run extraction, then confirm the draft.

Only **OWNER** and **ADMIN** members can edit branding. The controls live on the legal entity detail in **Settings → Legal Entities**, not on a separate gear tab.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Legal Entities">
    Click the gear → **Legal Entities**. Select the entity row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Document branding">
    Scroll to the **Document branding** section on the same page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload letterhead source">
    Attach a **letterhead** file (PNG or PDF, max 10 MiB). Wait until validation completes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Extract and confirm">
    When the source is valid, start letterhead extraction if offered. Review the proposal, save changes, then **Confirm** the branding draft. Discard the draft if you uploaded the wrong file.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Confirmed branding applies to PDFs for that legal entity going forward. Keep a backup of your source file outside ACP.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Gotchas">
    - Branding is per legal entity, not per site. Sites inherit the entity seller identity on invoices.
    - Extraction proposes layout bands from your upload; always eyeball a real PDF before you rely on it with customers.
    - A completed draft cannot be edited in place the same way as a draft — discard and re-upload if you need a full reset.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
