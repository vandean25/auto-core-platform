---
title: "Customers"
description: "Open a private or company record before you write an order or start a job."
---

**Customers** is the CRM list. Click a row for a **peek** side sheet, then **Open** for the detail card; more actions (e.g. **Delete**) sit behind **⋯** (orders and vehicles hang off that person). Top-right: **+ Customer**. Search: **Search customers...** Right-click **Delete** when the record has no orders, invoices, jobs, or vehicles.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create">
    Choose private vs company in the dialog. Company uses **company name**. Private uses first and last name. Address, email, and phone live on the same form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the row">
    Detail shows history. The **Vehicles** tab lists cars with **Role** and **Validity** when the vehicle has multiple parties (owner, driver, bill-to, and so on). From here you start a sales order with the customer already selected (`/sales-orders/new?customerId=…`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email for order communication">
    On the customer record and at workshop intake, confirm **Email address confirmed for order communication** when the customer gave an email for this repair (estimate, ready-for-pickup notice). This is not marketing consent. Without it, estimate and ready emails stay blocked on the order with a visible reason.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact preferences">
    Open the **Contact preferences** tab. Per legal entity you see channel, purpose, and whether contact is allowed. **Record marketing consent** or **Revoke marketing** appends to the **consent history** (text version and the wording shown to the customer). Outbound messages enforce these settings — marketing without consent is not sent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Legacy numbers">
    After [data import](/en/settings/data-import), **Legacy customer no. (source system)** appears read-only on the detail card (per import source). **Search customers...** and [Global search](/en/workflows/dashboard) match those IDs too.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Language for PDFs and email">
    Estimate, ready-for-pickup, and invoice PDFs plus related customer emails use the customer’s stored preferred language when set — otherwise the tenant default (usually German). You cannot edit language on the customer form today; import or integrations may set it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Warning>
Delete fails if the customer still owns a sales order, invoice, workshop order, or vehicle. The toast shows the server message.
</Warning>
