---
title: "Tyre storage"
description: "Store customer tyre sets, record tyre measurements, and check their condition at seasonal changeover."
---

**Tyre storage** tracks tyre sets per customer vehicle: storage location, check-in and check-out, and due dates for seasonal changeover. The **Tyre report** shows the latest recorded tyre measurement with an evaluation for each wheel.

Owners, administrators, and advisors can check sets in and out. Mechanics can view tyre sets, inspect their condition, and record measurements, but cannot check sets in or out.

| Task | Where you work |
| --- | --- |
| Check a tyre set in, check it out, or inspect it | Tyre storage, in the opened tyre set |
| Measure tyres on the vehicle | Workshop order or vehicle check-in, in **2 · Condition** |

## Record a tyre set and measurement

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Tyre storage">
    Sidebar → **Tyre storage**. Open an existing row or start a new set from the customer vehicle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a storage action or inspection">
    Use **Check in** with a storage location or **Check out** when the customer takes the tyres back. For a condition-only inspection, choose **Record tyre measurement**; the location and set status stay unchanged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable measurement capture">
    Select **Record tyre measurement** in the dialog. Readings are optional for check-in, check-out, and inspection; leaving this option off keeps the existing workflow. The set must be linked to a vehicle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Measure the wheels and save">
    Enter **Inner**, **Middle**, and **Outer** readings for each wheel as numbers from 0 to 20 mm. Add **Brand**, **Model**, **Size**, **Season**, **DOT**, **Construction**, and **Winter marking**, plus the vehicle's **Weight class**. **Save measurement** saves the measurement together with the chosen action.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Wheel positions are **Front left**, **Front right**, **Rear left**, and **Rear right**. **Record spare wheel** adds the fifth wheel if needed. **Copy brand, model and size to all wheels** copies only those details, not tread readings.

<Warning>
You cannot check in the same tyres twice without checking them out in between. Move actions do not record tyre measurements.
</Warning>

## Measure tyres on a workshop order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workshop order">
    Open the [Workshop order](/en/workflows/workshop-orders) and its **Tyre report** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Record tyre measurement">
    Choose **Record tyre measurement**. The action is also available through **Cmd+K**. In vehicle check-in, the tyre section is in **2 · Condition**; check-in keeps its three steps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the readings">
    Complete the wheel details as above and choose **Save measurement**. Record a correction as a new measurement; existing measurements are not overwritten.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the tyre report

The tyre report shows status and reasons for each wheel, the age derived from its DOT code, and highlights the lowest tread reading. The lowest of a wheel's three measurement points determines its evaluation. Missing details appear as a dash — they are neither 0 mm nor an estimated manufacturing date.

<Warning>
An amber evaluation is always a **recommendation**, not a legal limit. Tyre-age notices are also recommendations and do not by themselves mean a legal ban. Read the reasons and thresholds for the individual measurement.
</Warning>

## Connection and tyre photos

<Tip>
**Tyre measurement capture currently requires a connection.** Measurement drafts are not saved offline or automatically sent later. Separately, vehicle check-in photos can remain in the existing offline photo queue. Wait until the associated tyre photos have uploaded before saving the measurement.
</Tip>

Tyre photos can only be attached to the workshop order's matching vehicle check-in. An order without a vehicle check-in has no tyre photo support. Tyre photos on an order are currently unavailable in mechanic access. Tyre photos are currently unavailable for tyre storage check-in, check-out, or inspection — even with a linked workshop order.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Older measurements and DOT codes">
    Older measurements may contain only a middle tread reading; missing inner and outer readings appear as a dash. Old three-digit DOT codes provide only a minimum age, not an exact manufacturing year. Without a DOT code, age appears as a dash. If no evaluation is available for the country, readings remain visible without applying another country's evaluation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Due lists">
    Dashboard widgets and list filters highlight sets due for seasonal changeover. Pair this with [Workshop orders](/en/workflows/workshop-orders) when you book the appointment.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Storage locations">
    Bin codes come from [Storage locations](/en/settings/storage-locations). Create the structure before assigning precise bin positions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
