---
title: "Stock transfers"
description: "Move parts between sites with a tracked transfer document (Umlagerung)."
---

**Transfers** (*Umlagerungen*) move quantity from one site’s storage location to another. Each transfer has a status trail so parts clerks know what is in transit.

Parts staff, advisors, and admins create transfers. Mechanics consume stock through job issues rather than transfers.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Transfers">
    Sidebar → **Transfers**, or **Ctrl+K → Open Transfers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a transfer">
    Pick **from** site/location and **to** site/location. Add lines with SKU and quantity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ship and receive">
    Mark dispatched quantities when the pallet leaves. Complete receipt at the destination site to post inventory.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
You need an active **site** in the header switcher for operational stock views. See [Sites](/en/workflows/sites).
</Check>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Inventory detail">
    On-hand per bin still lives in [Inventory](/en/workflows/inventory). Transfers adjust quantities only when receipt completes.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
