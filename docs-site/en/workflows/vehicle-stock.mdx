---
title: "Vehicle stock"
description: "Buy, hold, and sell dealer vehicles with days-in-stock and margin visibility."
---

**Vehicle stock** is your forecourt ledger: purchase intake, standing time, margin, photos, equipment, and handover to a sale contract.

Sales, advisors, and admins run stock daily. Mechanics may view vehicles linked to jobs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Vehicle Stock">
    Sidebar → **Vehicle Stock** (*Fahrzeugbestand*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record a purchase">
    **New purchase** captures seller, purchase price, NoVA class, and acquisition date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enrich the unit">
    On the stock detail, add photos, equipment, and listing text before you advertise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sell the vehicle">
    Start **Sale** to generate the purchase contract PDF, trade-in lines, and statutory warranty term for [Statutory warranty due](/en/workflows/statutory-warranty-due).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Listings export" icon="share-2" href="/en/workflows/listings-export">
    Push CSV/XML to mobile.de, willhaben, or AutoScout24.
  </Card>
  <Card title="NoVA" icon="calculator" href="/en/workflows/nova">
    Preview tax before you price the car.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
Reports on standing time and margin live on the stock reports view when your tenant uses multi-site stock — filter by site in the header switcher.
</Tip>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Customer vehicles vs stock">
    The **Vehicles** module is customer-owned cars. **Vehicle stock** is inventory you intend to resell. A trade-in can link both records on the sale screen.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
