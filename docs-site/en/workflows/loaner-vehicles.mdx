---
title: "Loaner vehicles"
description: "Manage the courtesy car fleet and book a loaner against a workshop order."
---

**Loaner vehicles** (*Ersatzfahrzeuge*) lists your courtesy fleet. Book a vehicle to a workshop order so the job card and billing can show loaner dates and fee lines.

All standard workshop roles can browse the fleet. Booking details are usually handled by advisors and admins.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Loaner vehicles">
    Sidebar → **Loaner vehicles**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a fleet vehicle">
    Use **Add loaner vehicle**, pick the underlying vehicle record, and set a display name (for example “Golf loaner”).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book on a workshop order">
    From the order, assign a loaner and the until-date. The order header can show **Loaner:** with the label and return date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Print handover">
    Use the loaner print view when you need a paper handover document for the customer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Loaner days can post as fee line items on the workshop invoice. Confirm pricing with your [labor](/en/settings/labor) and revenue setup before you invoice.
</Tip>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can use this">
    The sidebar module is visible to all tenant roles. Fleet edits and bookings follow normal workshop order permissions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
