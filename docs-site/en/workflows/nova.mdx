---
title: "NoVA calculator"
description: "Preview Austrian NoVA on vehicle purchase and stock with the built-in calculation kernel."
---

The **NoVA** (*Normverbrauchsabgabe*) preview helps you estimate tax on vehicles you buy or sell, including EU used imports. It is a planning aid — always reconcile with your tax advisor before you contract.

Advisors, sales, and admins use the calculator on vehicle forms and stock purchase flows. The preview calls `POST /api/vehicles/nova/calculate` with your inputs.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a vehicle or stock purchase">
    Start from [Vehicles](/en/workflows/vehicles) or [Vehicle stock](/en/workflows/vehicle-stock) purchase intake.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set NoVA class">
    Choose **NoVA class** (for example **STANDARD**, **ELECTRIC_EXEMPT**, **OTHER**, or **NOT_SET**) on the vehicle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the preview panel">
    Enter net price and consumption or CO₂ inputs as the form asks. Click the calculate action in the **NoVA** preview panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use listing export">
    When you publish stock, confirm **NoVA included** flags on marketplace copy — see [Listings export](/en/workflows/listings-export).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
ACP shows limitation hints when inputs fall outside validated bands. Do not treat the preview as a filed tax return.
</Warning>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who can use this">
    Any role that can edit vehicle economics sees the preview. Final numbers on customer contracts still follow your legal entity settings.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
