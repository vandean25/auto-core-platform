---
title: "Pickerl due"
description: "Track §57a inspection (Pickerl) due dates and spin up inspection jobs."
---

**Pickerl due** lists vehicles whose periodic §57a inspection (*Pickerl*) is coming up or overdue. Austrian workshops use it to call customers and pre-build inspection orders.

All roles see the sidebar entry. Recording results and importing inspection outcomes is usually advisor or admin work.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Pickerl due">
    Sidebar → **Pickerl due** (*Pickerl*).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the list">
    Sort and filter by due date, customer, or plate. The [dashboard](/en/workflows/dashboard) can show a Pickerl widget when configured.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record inspection on the vehicle">
    On the vehicle detail, use the Pickerl section to set last result and next due date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a workshop order">
    Use one-click order creation from the due list when offered, then complete the inspection in the workshop.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
You can import inspection results in bulk when your test equipment exports compatible data. Use the import dialog on the vehicle or due list context where the app exposes it.
</Tip>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Vehicle master data">
    Plate and registration details live on [Vehicles](/en/workflows/vehicles). Pickerl due dates depend on accurate vehicle records.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
