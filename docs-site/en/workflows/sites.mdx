---
title: "Sites and site switcher"
description: "Work across workshops with site-scoped data and the header site switcher."
---

A **site** is one workshop location under your tenant. Stock, workshop orders, invoices, and many lists respect the **current site** you pick in the header **Switch site** control (*Standort*).

Day-to-day users switch sites. **OWNER** and **ADMIN** members configure sites under **Settings → Sites**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch site">
    In the header, open **Switch site** → **Activate** the site you are standing in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work in scoped modules">
    Inventory, workshop board, and transfers show data for the active site (and sites you are a member of).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure sites (admin)">
    Gear → **Sites**: create the site, link a [legal entity](/en/settings/legal-entities), and assign team members.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Operational views need a site selection. If you see **No active site**, pick one or ask an admin for membership.
</Warning>

<Tip>
Accounting export and export readiness may refuse periods when an inactive site still holds documents. Reactivate the site or move documents before you export.
</Tip>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Settings detail">
    Field-level site setup is documented in [Sites settings](/en/settings/sites).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
