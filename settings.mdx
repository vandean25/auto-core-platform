title: "Settings"
description: "Every workshop control plane lives behind the gear: books, people, bins, and the audit trail."
---

Click the **gear** in the app header. Settings is one tabbed page for this tenant (17 tabs). There is no separate admin site. Several tabs (legal entities, sites, team, API keys, margin rules, data import, and the Finance **Accounting export** sub-tab) are visible only to **OWNER** and **ADMIN**.

<Tip>
  **Employees** are workshop roster names. **Team** members are login accounts. Mixing them up is the usual first mistake.
