---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Open ACP, do one real job, then confirm the change landed in the audit trail."
---

This is the shortest path from login to a record you can prove.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to your workshop tenant">
    Use the account your administrator invited. ACP isolates every tenant. You only see your workshop's customers, jobs, and stock.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete one business change">
    Edit a customer, confirm a sales order, update a workshop task, or receive a purchase. The product already records the mutation. You do not turn auditing on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the audit trail">
    Go to **Settings → Audit Logs**. Search for the person, document id, or request id. Click the row to see before, after, and the field-level diff.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Check>
If the row is missing, the change was likely a create (new records are the first state) or a model that is not on the audited list. See [Audit logs](/settings/audit-logs).
</Check>
