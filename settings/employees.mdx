---
title: "Employees"
description: "Manage the workshop roster, then open HR for attendance and leave."
---

Use **Settings → Employees** for the workshop roster: mechanics, service advisors, and parts clerks. This is the same employee list shown in [HR](/hr/employees). These are **not** login accounts. Logins live under [Team](/settings/team).

Search box: **Search employees...** Top-right: **+ Employee**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create">
    **+ Employee** → **Create Employee**. Name is required. Role defaults to Mechanic. Sort order must be a whole number ≥ 0.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit in the row">
    Click the name or sort order to save on blur. Role and mother language are dropdowns. They save as soon as you pick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Active / Inactive">
    The **Active** / **Inactive** button toggles assignment eligibility without removing the row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open HR details">
    Open [HR → Employees](/hr/employees) for hire date, leave allowance, remaining leave, login links, and **Carryover this year**. Owners and administrators can save the leave balance there.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Columns

| Column | How you change it |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Inline edit |
| **Role** | Mechanic, Service Advisor, Parts Clerk |
| **Mother Language** | Default (target language), or a spoken locale such as German (Germany) |
| **Active** | Toggle button |
| **Sort Order** | Inline edit, whole number ≥ 0 |

Right-click a row → **Delete**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Unused employee">
    Delete can remove the row. Toast: **Employee deleted**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Assigned to a job">
    Delete is blocked or becomes a deactivate. Toast: **Employee deactivated**. Prefer the Active toggle when they still exist on the board.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Tip>
**Mother Language** is what they speak. **Target Language** on [Voice translation](/settings/voice-translation) is what the office reads. Leave mother language on Default unless this person speaks something else. For attendance and leave, use [HR](/hr), not the workshop board.
</Tip>
