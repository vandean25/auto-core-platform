---
title: "Revenue groups"
description: "Name the tax buckets your invoices and catalog items report into."
---

Use **Settings → Revenue Groups** to tell the accountant which sales are parts, which are labor, and which tax rate and ledger account they use.

The group **name** is copied onto invoice lines at sale time. Renaming a group later does not rewrite old invoices.

<Info>
The table is a list. There is no row click to edit, and no delete on this screen. You add groups with **Group** (plus icon) at the top right of the card.
</Info>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Revenue Groups">
    Gear in the header, then the **Revenue Groups** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the table">
    Columns are **Name**, **Tax Rate**, **Account Number**, and **Default**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a group when you need a new tax bucket">
    Click **Group**. Fill the dialog. **Create Group** writes it for this tenant.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fields you fill

| Field | Example | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Name** | Spare Parts 20% | Shown on the group list and snapshotted onto invoice lines |
| **Tax Rate (%)** | 20.00 | VAT percent for items that use this group |
| **Account Number** | 4000 | Mapping for the accountant / export |
| **Set as default for new items** | checked or not | New catalog items can pick this group first |

One row can show a green **Default** badge. Others show a dash.

<Warning>
You cannot delete a revenue group from this tab. Backend rules still block a delete when a catalog item still points at the group. Historical invoices keep the old **name** even if you later add a replacement group.
</Warning>

<Accordion title="Where the group is used">
Catalog items and labor carry a revenue group. When an invoice is issued, the line stores the group **name** and unit price as they were at that moment. Dashboard revenue charts group finalized invoice lines by that snapshotted name for the current month.
</Accordion>
