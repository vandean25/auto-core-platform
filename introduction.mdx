---
title: "Introduction"
description: "ACP is the workshop ERP for parts, jobs, invoices, and the people who change them."
---

ACP keeps a workshop running as one system: stock on the shelf, jobs on the board, bills from vendors, and invoices to customers.

Every business change is treated like a ledger entry. You can later answer *who changed this, when, and what it looked like before*.
